Tindahan Blocks Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Short version: no account, no sign-in, and your game stays on your iPhone. The game is free because it shows ads from Google, and Google's ad tools collect some device data to show them.

Who we are

Tindahan Blocks (“the app”, “we”, “our”) is a block-puzzle game for iPhone, made by Mark Raphael Sto. Domingo. This page explains what the app keeps, what Google's ad tools collect, and the choices you have.

No account

There is no account and no sign-in. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location. We don't run a server that stores information about you.

Game data stays on your iPhone

The app saves its data only on your device, using iOS's on-device storage:

  • Progress: best score, coins, unlocked levels, your current round and daily orders.
  • Settings: haptics on or off, and whether ads are removed.
  • Ad timing: simple counters (such as sessions played and when an ad last showed) so ads stay infrequent.

We never receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads from Google (AdMob)

The app shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). You choose when to watch a rewarded ad (for example, to save a run or get an extra undo), and the app may show a full-screen ad between store days. Google is the only ad company the app uses.

To show and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers. On iPhone this includes the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking (see below).
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown and whether you tapped them.
  • Approximate location worked out from your IP address, used to pick ads and decide which consent rules apply where you are. Not your precise location.
  • Diagnostics: crash and performance data about the ads.

The app doesn't link this data to your name or any account, because there isn't one. Google handles it under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

iOS asks whether the app may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. Before that, the app shows a short note explaining why. If you tap Allow, Google can use your IDFA to show more personalized ads. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and you still get ads, just less tailored ones. The game plays the same either way.

You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Ad consent (Google consent form)

In some regions, such as the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form the first time you open it. You choose whether Google may use your data for personalized ads. If consent can't be confirmed, the app asks for non-personalized ads or shows no ads at all, and play goes on.

To change your choice later, open the app's Settings and tap Privacy choices (shown where the consent form applies).

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.
  • No selling of your data.
  • No collecting of names, emails, contacts, photos or precise location.

If you remove ads with an in-app purchase, payment goes through Apple. We don't see your payment details.

Children

Tindahan Blocks is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child's data has been collected through the app, email us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
